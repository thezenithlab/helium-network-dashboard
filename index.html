Data limitation: hotspot deactivations after the Solana migration
Helium's Solana entity manager records onboarding events only. There is no on-chain deactivation instruction, so hotspot churn after April 2023 cannot be recovered from blockchain data at all. Counts below are therefore cumulative registrations, not active devices, and the two diverge widely. The only direct measure of active devices is an oracle snapshot, currently two readings 13 days apart (7 April 2026: 207,332; 20 April 2026: 204,389). Treat it as a point-in-time reading, not a trend.

How to read the coverage figures
Coverage is the share of the 1,936 daily rows in which a column has a value. Low coverage reflects limits of the upstream public sources, not gaps in collection: free-tier APIs cap history at 365 days, Helium's metrics feed serves a rolling 182-day window, and the oracle device counts are point-in-time readings rather than a series. Sparse fields are kept rather than filled with modelled values, so that what was actually observed stays auditable.
